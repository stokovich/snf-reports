How it is calculated. Output and input of the production lines as Refined PrUn's XIT BURN does it: the queued orders of every line (only the recurring ones when the member has any), each as amount × capacity ÷ their total time in days. Workforce: the needs FIO reports for the base; for a member who does not share workforce with the group it is calculated from the workers the buildings require.
Net = output − input − workforce. Positive is a surplus, negative a deficit. The threshold hides materials where no member reaches that value.
